    Our Story

    Built from Classroom Frustration. Growing Through Innovation.

    SchoolHouse Logistics was founded out of a simple, persistent frustration: schools were overwhelmed with paperwork, confusing workflows, and disconnected systems. As a teacher trying to manage courses, activities, communication, and daily responsibilities, I saw firsthand how much valuable time was being lost to tasks that should have been streamlined or automated.

    Tired of the clutter, I began experimenting with VibeCoding and other AI-assisted tools. My original goal was not to start a company. It was simply to solve the problems happening in my own classroom. I created early prototypes to organize workflows, simplify scheduling, and reduce repetitive administrative work. As I shared these ideas with other teachers, I quickly realized something: many educators were fighting the exact same battles, and these challenges could be solved much more easily than anyone expected.

    That realization became the spark for SchoolHouse Logistics.

    What started as small, experimental solutions grew into a clear and larger vision. As more teachers expressed the need for these tools, SchoolHouse Logistics took shape as a company dedicated to improving the way schools operate. Our work is grounded in real classroom experience, real educator feedback, and a commitment to designing tools that make school life easier.

    Today, SchoolHouse Logistics is building a dedicated internal development team made up of engineers, designers, and product thinkers who work closely with educators to create dependable and thoughtfully designed applications within the SchoolOS ecosystem. By moving from quick prototypes to fully engineered products, we are building tools that are stable, scalable, and ready for long-term use in schools.

    Our mission remains consistent:

    Remove unnecessary complexity so educators can focus on students, not paperwork.

    SchoolHouse Logistics began in a classroom with a simple idea.
    Now it is evolving into a team-driven organization committed to transforming school operations one workflow at a time.

    Our Approach

    We're a small, focused operation with low overhead and honest pricing. That means no hidden fees, no up-selling, and no pressure. Our prices are listed clearly, and if applications work well together, we offer them in discounted packages.

    Our support is responsive, and our tools are built for organizations like yours—not some idealized version of one. Every application we build comes from first-hand experience and pain points we've felt ourselves, streamlined into software that just works.

    We're not trying to reinvent operations. We're just trying to make them a little easier to manage, one application at a time.
